PhD research title

Nanotechnologies and sensors for occupational health and safety in exposure to hazardous substances “Nanotecnologie e sensori per la salute e sicurezza sul lavoro nell’esposizione a sostanze pericolose”

Research topic

Development of nanotechnology-based sensing for workers exposed to hazardous airborne substances, within an industrial research project funded by INAIL. The work combines wearable particulate-matter sensors with CFD simulation of particle transport in workplaces (laboratories, factories) to estimate the presence of ultrafine and nanoparticles (<100 nm) that standard optical sensors cannot detect directly.

About PM Monitor

PM Monitor is the measurement platform of this PhD project: wearable PM boxes connected by Bluetooth or USB, real-time exposure statistics and alarms, GPS tracking of the worker’s path, and CFD-based estimation of nanoparticle concentration for specific materials and workplace geometries.
